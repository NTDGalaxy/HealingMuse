# HealingMuse

HealingMuse is a Vietnamese-first, AI-free music and emotional wellbeing PWA for adults living with cancer.
It offers recreation and companionship without diagnostic or treatment claims.
People can browse and listen without an account.
An account enables favourites, playlists and optional private mood check-ins.

## Catalogue

The initial preparation target is 1,000 distinct human-created instrumental and nature recordings.
Discovery and delivery support configurable targets through 10,000.
A recording becomes public only after a content administrator confirms provenance, distribution rights and personal listening review.
Preparation, approval and publication are reported separately.
Copies, trimmed versions and repeated loops do not count as new originals.
Technical checks never stand in for subjective human listening.

## Discovery

The home screen offers actionable music choices in the first phone viewport.
Search and filters live in the URL.
Published discovery pages contain 24 recordings by default and never more than 48.
Favourites, playlists and curated collections resolve tracks independently of the visible page.
The player stays mounted across navigation and extends browse queues incrementally.

## Privacy and access

Mood records belong to their owner and are never available to content administrators.
The service worker caches only the explicitly allowed public offline shell.
Only an explicit user download with compatible distribution rights may persist audio locally.
Personal mutations are never queued offline.
Native semantics, visible focus, reduced motion and touch targets of at least 44 CSS pixels are required.
Automated accessibility checks are reported separately from actual assistive technology and physical-device tests.
